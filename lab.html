The Lab

Cabin

A Bandcamp player for Mac and iPhone, written in Swift. It turns the albums you’ve bought on Bandcamp into a collection you actually want to browse: flipped through like records in a crate, read like liner notes, and mapped by where the music comes from.

Try Cabin (opens in new tab)

Mac & iPhone

Crate

Your collection as a stack of records, flicked through one sleeve at a time: a shelf of covers on Mac, a stack under your thumb on iPhone. Filter by New, Favourites, Downloaded or Imported, or switch to the grid, playlists and wishlist.

iPhone

Album page

Tracks first, then the liner notes: how often you’ve played it, the album’s story, where the artist is from, the receipt of your purchase and full credits.

Mac & iPhone

Now Playing

The artist, the album, its credits and synced lyrics around the artwork: side by side on Mac, a swipe away on iPhone, with the queue and AirPlay one tap off.

iPhone

Collection map

Every album pinned to where the artist is from, across your collection, your wishlist or your friends’. Open any place to see what’s from there, or start a radio of it.

Mac

Recap

Your year or month on Bandcamp: a cloud of the covers you played, the artists you supported, your sound by genre and a day-by-day calendar, saved as a playlist in one click.
